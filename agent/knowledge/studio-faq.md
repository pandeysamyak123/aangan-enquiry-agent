# Aangan Studio — Frequently asked questions

**Who are you?**
Aangan Studio is an interior design studio in Pune, founded by Nikhil Deshpande. We design and execute homes and small offices across Pune city and PCMC, with a team of 14 designers and our own network of contractors and vendors.

**Is the first consultation free?**
Yes. The first consultation is free and there is no obligation.

**Can the designer come to my site?**
Yes. For most projects the designer prefers to see the site, including new-possession flats where the builder can let us in.

**How long does a project take?**
Design takes about 3–4 weeks from the first consultation. Execution takes 8–16 weeks depending on size and how ready the site is. The designer confirms the timeline for your project at the consultation.

**Can you do it before a festival / in three weeks?**
Usually not. We can't begin execution on a project that needs to be ready in under 6 weeks, and a single room with full execution takes about 8–10 weeks end to end. We'd rather say so honestly than rush it. Starting after the date is often the best option.

**I'm renting. Can you still do my flat?**
Yes, as long as no structural changes are needed. Modular kitchens can be removed cleanly, and flooring, lighting and furniture are reversible. It helps to have the landlord's agreement.

**Do you do just one room?**
Yes, if it's a complete redesign of that room with materials, furniture and execution. We don't do advice-only or styling-only visits.

**Do you do offices?**
Yes: offices, clinics and studios up to about 3,000 sq ft, including workstations, cabins, meeting rooms, reception and common areas.

**What don't you do?**
Restaurants, hotels, retail stores and gyms; architecture or structural work (moving walls, permits); colour or furniture advice on its own; furniture sourcing without a design project; standalone Vastu consultation (we include Vastu inside our designs); and projects outside Pune and PCMC.

**Can I see your work?**
Our portfolio is on our website and Instagram. Your designer can also show relevant past projects at the consultation.

**Do you work with Vastu?**
Yes, we incorporate Vastu requirements into our designs. We don't offer Vastu advice as a separate service.
