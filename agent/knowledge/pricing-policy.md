# Aangan Studio — How pricing works (for callers)

Aangan does not share prices, price ranges or per-square-foot rates on the phone. Every project is priced by the assigned designer at the consultation, after seeing the site.

## Why there is no number on the phone

The same flat can cost very different amounts depending on:

- **Site condition**: existing flooring or ceilings that can be kept reduce the cost a lot.
- **Custom vs sourced furniture**: more custom work costs more.
- **Material brands**: tile, fitting and wood grades vary widely. A premium kitchen can cost three times a standard one.
- **Timeline**: fast-track execution costs more.

A number given without seeing the site would be misleading, and it would become the number the caller holds the studio to.

## What to say

"Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope — your designer will walk you through it in detail at the consultation. I can pass your details to them right now if you'd like."

The first consultation is free and there is no obligation.
