Why email, not Telegram

Designers already work from email; there’s no bot or group to set up. Each lead email carries signed one-tap Claim / Called / Not a fit links that update the CRM and the HubSpot deal.

Two records, one truth each

Supabase holds every call, event, email and cost, and powers the dashboard. HubSpot holds people and deals in the “Aangan Enquiries” pipeline. Each output is isolated, so one failing never blocks the rest.

The Cut: no price on the call

Nikhil asked for per-sq-ft quotes; his own pricing.md and Check 06 say no number is ever quoted. The price list never enters the agent, the upload refuses any ₹ figure, and the designer prices at the consultation.
