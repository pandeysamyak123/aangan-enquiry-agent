Why these choices

  • Email over Telegram. Designers already live in email, it needs no bot or group, the invite and the lead arrive together, and signed links give one-tap Claim without an app.
  • HubSpot as the sales record, Supabase as the call record. HubSpot holds people and deals; Supabase holds every call, event and cost so the dashboard never depends on CRM limits.
  • Rules re-check the AI. A wrong decline is a lost ₹8–14 lakh project; a second opinion costs nothing.

The Cut: no price on the call

Nikhil asked for per-sq-ft quotes. His own pricing.md says no number is ever quoted, and Check 06 (failure risk) agrees: a number in the studio’s voice becomes a commitment.

So the price list never enters the agent, the budget judgement runs on the server, and pricing stays with the designer at the consultation.
